Added by issue #33 — part of the browse/re-open pair ADR 020 item 6 shipped with no reference. Built and real. What this page is for is worth stating, because the mockup itself is nowhere on it: ADR 020 item 2 makes the repository the source of truth for the artifact, so this page answers "what happened to this design?" from the designs table and the session list, and the mockup is viewed through the draft PR. That is why the primary action re-opens a session rather than previewing the HTML. The session list is the point of the page: a design's own status records only whether it was ever committed, while whether it is being worked on *right now* is the newest session's business, so the two are shown separately and the footnote below the list says so. Rows are sample data. The design's /designs/sessions/:sessionId transcript view has no wireframe yet — the "Open transcript" buttons here are inert for that reason.
