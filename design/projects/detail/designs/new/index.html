Built and real. The two headings, the pre-filled name, the designs/<slug>/ sentence and the disabled-until-ready gate all come from the component. What this state has to get right, and why it is worth a wireframe: the slug is carried through, not re-derived from the name (ADR 020 item 2) — the slug is what selects the same designs/<slug>/ folder and the same index row, so a user who edits the name must still land in their existing folder rather than in a second one. The name therefore arrives pre-filled but the identity does not depend on it. Also not mocked: the create state (/designs/new with no ?reopen — "Start a design session", an empty form, and the same two warning lines), the two project-readiness warnings shown when status !== "ready" or the project has no design surface, and the submitting state. The badge/provenance variants of the form — a design session started from a design_grill Action Item or from a feature (featureId / actionItemId) — look identical here and are not distinguished in the UI.
