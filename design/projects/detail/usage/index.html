Wireframe · maps to /projects/:projectId/usage — a new route/page, no equivalent exists in web/ today. Same "entirely speculative" caveat as /usage (see its design-note) — no token-tracking concept exists anywhere in the current system. This project-level view is deliberately framed as a slice of the organization's quota, not a separate one: providers/limits/reset dates are owned by Acme Retail (org-managed since /settings/organization), this project just shows how much of that shared quota its own jobs accounted for. No progress-bar-against-a-limit here on purpose — a per-project "limit" isn't a real thing unless the org configures per-project caps, which isn't a decided feature either.
