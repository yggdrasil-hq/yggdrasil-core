Wireframe · maps to /usage — a new route/page, no equivalent exists in web/ today. More speculative than Deployments was: there is no token-usage tracking concept anywhere in the current system — not in any ADR, not in job-dispatch.md, nothing. roadmap/phases.md Phase 4 lists "token budgets" as unbuilt, and that's a spending cap, not consumption reporting — a different feature. This whole page assumes the API/Orchestrator logs a token count per job and exposes it somewhere, which doesn't exist yet. Also worth being precise about: providers are bring-your-own-key and org-managed (per /settings/organization) — Yggdrasil doesn't bill or own a quota system, so any "resets in N days" shown here is the provider's own billing-cycle reset (e.g. OpenRouter's), only as good as whatever the provider's API reports back, not something Yggdrasil controls. Numbers, percentages, and reset dates below are entirely invented for the mockup. Project-level and account-level (user) versions of this page show the same providers scoped down to that project's/that user's share of consumption — see their own design-notes for how the three levels relate (org is the authoritative quota view; project and user are contribution slices of it, not separate quotas).
