Wireframe · maps to /deployments — a new route/page, no equivalent exists in web/ today. Same maturity caveat as the project-level version (/projects/:projectId/deployments, see its design-note): only Production rows reflect something actually built (ADR 003/ADR 013); Preview is designed but has zero orchestrator implementation (roadmap/phases.md, Phase 2); Staging isn't decided at all (open-questions.md #9). This page additionally assumes every project has at most one production deployment and that deployments are visible org-wide to every member regardless of role — the permission matrix on /settings/organization doesn't currently say who can *view* deployments vs. just projects in general, worth resolving alongside whichever ADR formalizes this page. Internal Ops Console and Data Pipeline are shown with empty states rather than invented deployments: the former is still initializing (ADR 002 §4 — a project can't do much until project_init merges), and the latter is an ETL project that may not expose a web app at all — not every project necessarily has something to deploy. Filter pills are inert.
