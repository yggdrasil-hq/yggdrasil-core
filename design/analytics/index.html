Wireframe · maps to /analytics — a new route/page, no equivalent exists in web/ today. Same root problem as /usage: no token-tracking or session-analytics concept exists anywhere in the system — this page assumes every job (spec_grill/feature_build/test_run, plus design_grill once ADR 014 ships) reports a token count and duration back to the API, persisted per-run, queryable by project/user/kind/date. None of that exists. The four "session types" shown (design session/spec grill/implementation/testing) map onto real job kinds — design_grill, spec_grill, feature_build, test_run — so that part is grounded in job-dispatch.md even though the analytics layer on top isn't. The heatmap is a GitHub-contribution-graph pattern applied to job-run activity, a full 52 weeks like the real thing — but unlike GitHub's fixed small squares, cells here are fluid (CSS Grid 1fr columns) so the whole graph genuinely fills the available width rather than sitting as a small fixed-size cluster on a wide page; cells render as rectangles rather than squares as a result, a deliberate trade for "spans full width" over "square cells." Cell values are randomly generated for the mockup, not derived from the (fake) session list below them, so don't expect the two to visually correlate. "By project," "by user," and the recent-sessions list are all invented sample data. Project-level and account-level (user) versions of this page drop the breakdown that would be redundant at that scope (project-level drops "by project," user-level drops "by user") — see their own design-notes.
